Shopify vs Etsy in the UK: What a £140 Order Really Costs

A £140 order is a good test size for a UK maker deciding between Etsy and their own Shopify store: big enough that the percentage fees bite, small enough that the monthly subscription still matters. Run both and the answer is clearer than either platform's marketing suggests.

Etsy in the UK takes its cut in five pieces. The transaction fee is 6.5% of the price plus shipping: £9.10. Payment processing is 4% plus £0.20: £5.80. The listing fee is £0.20, and the UK regulatory operating fee adds 0.48%: £0.67. Total: £15.77 on a £140 order, leaving £124.23. If the sale came through Offsite Ads, add 15% (£21.00) — or 12% (£16.80) for shops over $10,000 in the prior year — which can quietly turn a good order into a thin one.

Shopify UK charges a flat £25 a month for the Basic plan, plus 2% plus 25p per order: £3.05 on a £140 order. The saving against Etsy is £12.72 per order, so the subscription pays for itself at about 3 orders a month. Below that, Etsy's per-order model is still cheaper; above it, your own store keeps more of every sale.
